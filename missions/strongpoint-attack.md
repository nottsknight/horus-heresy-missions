# Strongpoint Attack

The attacker in this mission is assaulting a fortified position held
by the enemy. They must destroy the enemy defences to make way for
a larger assault force.

## Victory conditions

The following Mission Objectives are used in this Mission:

### Primary Mission Objectives

- Attacker: Destroy Bunkers
- Defender: Hold the Line

### Secondary Mission Objectives

- Slay the Warlord (2)
- Giant Killer (2)
- Last Man Standing (4)
- First Strike (2)

In this mission no Objective Markers are placed. Instead, after the
Select Deployment Map of the Core Mission Sequence, the defender chooses
one Fortification unit with the Bunker subtype to be the Strongpoint.

**Destroy Bunkers**
The attacker gains 6 Victory Points for destroying the Strongpoint. Units
with the Vanguard (X) special rule score X additional Victory Points if they
are responsible for removing the Strongpoint's final Hull Point.

**Hold the Line**
The defender scores 2 Victory Points at the end of each of their turns if
they have a unit embarked inside the Strongpoint. A unit with the Line (X)
special rule scores X additional victory points if they are the unit
embarked.

## Mission Special Rules

This mission uses the **Strongpoint Attack Mission**, **Obstacles**, **Sentries**,
**Reserves**, and **Counter Offensive** Mission Special Rules.

### Strongpoint Attack Mission

In this mission the defender is holding a fortified position from the
attacker. This has the following effects:

- In the Select Armies step, the defender gains an additional 200pts in
  their army. These additional points may only be spent on units with the
  Fortification Battlefield Role, and they must select at least one unit with
  the Bunker Sub-Type to serve as the Strongpoint.
- Skip the Select Deployment Map step. Instead, nominate a point on one of the
  long table edges. The defender's deployment zone forms a 24x24" square area
  centred on that point.
- In the Declare Mission Reserves step, the defender must place all units that
  do not have the High Command, Command, Troops, Support, or Armour Battlefield
  Roles in reserve.
- In the Deploy Armies step, the defender deploys first. They first deploy Sentries
  anywhere on the table outside their deployment zone, and then deploy their army.
  After the defender has deployed, the attacker deploys anywhere on the table
  more than 18" away from the defender's deployment zone.
