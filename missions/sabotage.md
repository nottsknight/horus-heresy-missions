# Sabotage

In this mission the attacker is attempting to raid a particularly
important installation belonging to the defender. They must avoid
detection for as long as possible.

## Victory conditions

The following Mission Objectives are used in this Mission:

### Primary Mission Objectives

- Attacker: Destroy the Installation
- Defender: Protect the Installation

### Secondary Mission Objectives

- None

This mission does not use Victory Points. Instead, the entire goal
is to protect or destroy the installation:

**Destroy the Installation**
To achieve this Objective, the attacker must destroy the installation
using their demolition charges (see below). If this is achieved, the
game immediately ends and the attacker wins.

**Protect the Installation**
To achieve this Objective, the defender must prevent the installation
from being destroyed. If the installation is still intact at the end
of the game, the defender wins.

## Mission Special Rules

This Mission uses the **Demolition Charges**, **Sabotage Mission**,
**Reserves**, and **Sentries** Mission Special Rules.

### Demolition Charges

Every unit in the attacker's army is equipped with special demolition
charges for destroying the installation. These charges may only be
used against the installation, not against any other unit.

Demolition charges are placed in the Combat Phase. A unit must be in
base contact with the installation to attempt to place charges, but
because the installation is not an enemy unit it may be approached
freely. The unit attempting to place charges must pass an Intelligence
test; if passed, the charges are placed. There is no effect if the test
is failed.

Multiple attacking units may attempt to place charges on the same turn.
At the end of each of the attacker's turns, if they successfully placed
charges on the installation, the installation is destroyed.

### Sabotage Mission

In this mission the attacker is attempting to approach and destroy a
vital piece of enemy equipment. This has the following rules:

- During the Prepare the Battlefield step, the first terrain piece placed
  must be the installation. This is placed in the centre of the table, and
  should be around 2" square. Players may give whatever terrain rules to it
  that they deem appropriate.
- The Deploy Objectives step is skipped.
- The Select Deployment Map step is skipped. In this mission, the defender
  deploys within 8" of the installation, and the attacker deploys within 12"
  of one of the short table edges.
- During the Declare Mission Reserves step, the defender must place all units
  that do not have the Troops, Command, or High Command Battlefield Role
  in reserve.
- In the Deploy Armies step, the defender deploys first in their deployment
  zone. They may then deploy a number of sentries depending on ther faction,
  according to the Sentries Mission Special Rule. Sentries may be deployed
  anywhere on the table outside of the defender's deployment zone, and more
  than 12" from a short table edge.

  The attacker then chooses one of the short table edges and deploys their
  army within 12" of it.
