# King of the Hill

In this mission, armies clash over a strategically important resource.

## Victory conditions

The following Mission Objectives are used in this mission:

### Primary Mission Objectives

- This mission includes 1 objective worth 3 VP.

### Secondary Mission Objectives

- Slay the Warlord (2)
- Giant Killer (2)
- Last Man Standing (3)
- First Strike (3)


## Mission special rules

This mission uses the **King of the Hill Mission**, **Reserves**,
and **Counter Offensive** Mission Special Rules.

### Changing Priorities Mission

During setup, each player should place 1 objective in the centre of the battlefield.

A unit that is within 3" of the objective cannot make any reactions.
