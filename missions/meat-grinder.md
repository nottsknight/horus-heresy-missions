# Meat Grinder

One army has launched a brutal _coup de main_ attack across the
entire front, and in this sector the defender's only hope is to try
and hold out long enough for reinforcements to arrive.

## Victory conditions

The following Mission Objectives are used in this Mission:

### Primary Mission Objectives

- Attacker: Annihilate the Enemy
- Defender: Survive the Onslaught

### Secondary Mission Objectives

- None

This mission does not use Objective Markers. The two Primary Mission
Objectives are the only way to score points.

**Annihilate the Enemy**
At the end of the game, the attacker wins if there are no defending units
left on the table.

**Survive the Onslaught**
At the end of the game, the defender wins if they have any units left
on the table.

## Mission Special Rules

This mission uses the **Random Game Length** and **Sustained Attack**
Mission Special Rules.
