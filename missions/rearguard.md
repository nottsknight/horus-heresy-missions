# Rearguard

The defender's main army is in retreat, and it is up to the units
chosen to form the rearguard to slow the pursuing attackers down
long enough for the main force to reach safety.

## Victory condititions

The following Mission Objectives are used in this Mission:

### Primary Mission Objectives

- Hold the Line

### Secondary Mission Objectives

- None

**Hold the Line**
This mission does not use Victory Points. Instead, victory is determined
at the end of the battle. If, at the end of the battle, the defender has
at least one Surviving Unit that is in range of an enemy unit with any of
their weapons, then the defender wins. Otherwise, the attacker wins.

A Surviving Unit is defined as any vehicle unit that does not have a
Tactical Status, or any non-vehicle unit that is at 50% of its starting
strength or higher.

## Mission Special Rules

This mission uses the **Rearguard Mission**, **Hidden Deployment**, **Obstacles**,
**Random Game Length**, and **Reserves** Mission Special Rules.

### Rearguard Mission

The defender's task is merely to delay the attacker for as long as possible.
This has the following effects:

- Skip the Select Deployment Map step. Instead, the players roll off and the winner
  nominates which long board edge is the defender's. The defender's deployment zone
  extends from their board edge half way along the width of the table (i.e. 24" on
  a 6x4" table).
- In the Declare Mission Reserves step, both players must split their armies
  into two groups. The defender can split their force however they like so long
  as each group has at least one unit in it. The attacker must ensure that both groups
  have at least 1/3 of the total points value of their army.
- In the Deploy Armies step, the defender deploys first. They must flip a coin or
  roll a dice to determine which of their two groups takes part in the battle; the
  other group is not used. They then set up their army in their deployment zone using
  the rules for Hidden Deployment.

  The attacker's army does not start on the table. Once the defender has deployed,
  they select one of their army groups to form the First Wave; the other group forms
  the Second Wave.

- The attacker takes the first turn, and may move all units from the First Wave onto
  the battlefield from their table edge as if from reserves.
- The attacker's Second Wave automatically arrives at the start of turn 3.
