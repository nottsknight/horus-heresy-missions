# Take and Hold

In this mission, the attacker has been tasked with capturing a
strategically important location such as a building or prominent hill.
They must gain control of it a defend it from counter attack.

## Victory conditions

The following Mission Objectives are used in this mission:

### Primary Mission Objectives

- Hold the Point

### Secondary Mission Objectives

- Slay the Warlord (2)
- Giant Killer (2)
- Last Man Standing (4)
- First Strike (2)

This mission does not use Objective Markers. Instead, after deployment maps
have been determined but before deployment, the defender nominates one piece
of terrain to be an objective. This should be a piece of Area Terrain or a
Building so that both players may move units onto it.

**Hold the Point**
In the Victory Sub-Phase of each Player's turn as the Active Player they
may score Victory Points if they control the chosen terrain piece. Control of
the terrain piece is worked out in the same way as for Objective Markers,
except that units must be within the footprint of the terrain piece to be
counted; the 3" radius is not used. The chosen terrain piece is worth
3 Victory Points.

## Mission special rules

This mission uses the **Take and Hold Mission**, **Random Game Length**, **Reserves**,
and **Counter Offensive** Mission Special Rules.

### Take and Hold Mission

In this mission, the attacker is launching an assault on a partially-defended
location. This has the following effects:

- During the Deploy Objectives step, the defender chooses a terrain piece to
  serve as the objective as described above. This terrain piece must be at least
  24" from one of the two long table edges, and at least 12" from any other table
  edge.
- Skip the Select Deployment Map step. Instead, nominate the long table edge furthest
  from the objective terrain piece to be the defender's table edge. The opposite
  long edge becomes the attacker's table edge.
- During the Declare Mission Reserves step, the attacker must place their entire
  army in reserve. The defender must place all units that do not have the Troops,
  Support, or Armour Battlefield Role in reserve and may place any additional units
  in reserve if they wish.
- During the Deploy Armies step, the defender deploys their units on or within 12"
  of the terrain piece chosen to be the objective. The attacker does not deploy
  any funis.

The attacker automatically takes the first turn, and moves as much of their army
onto the table as they like as if from reserves. The game then proceeds as normal.
