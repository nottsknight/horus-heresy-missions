# Mission special rules

These special rules supplement the rules in the HH3e rulebook for added
flavour in certain missions.

## Preliminary bombardment

**Long-range artillery attempts to suppress the enemy before the battle.**

A mission that uses this special rule will specify which player(s) may
make a preliminary bombardment. The bombardment happens after each side
has deployed but before the first turn.

To make a preliminary bombardment, the player making the bombardment rolls
a D6 for each unit the enemy has deployed on the table. On a 6, that unit suffers
D6 automatic wounds. Armour saves may be taken as normal against these wounds.
In addition, a unit that is hit by a preliminary bombardment must pass a
Cool check or become Pinned.

## Sentries

**In certain missions the attacker must get as close to the objective
as possible without alerting the enemy.**

In a mission with the Sentries special rule, the defender may deploy a
number of additional models as sentries alongside whatever units from their
army they are allowed to deploy. The number and type of models depends on
the defender's primary faction:

- **Legio Custodes:** 3 Custodian Guard
- **Legiones Astartes:** 6 Tactical Marines
- **Solar Auxilia:** 10 Auxilia

At the start of the game, only the defender's sentries may move. The rest of
the defender's army may only be activated once the alarm has been sounded.

### Moving sentries

At the start of the defender's movement phase, both players roll a D6 for each
sentry. The winner of the roll can move that sentry in any direction following
the normal movement rules. Sentries may not Rush.

### Sounding the alarm

There are five ways the alarm can be sounded.

1. **Spotting distance:** If a sentry ends the movement phase within spotting
   distance of an enemy model, the alarm is sounded. A sentry's spotting distance
   is equal to its Initiative in inches.
2. **Vehicles:** If the attacker deploys any vehicles, bikes, jetbikes, dreadnoughts,
   aircraft, or other obviously noisy units onto the table, the alarm is
   immediately raised.
3. **Weapons fire:** If the attacker makes a shooting attack with any weapon that
   is not obviously silenced (e.g. a Nemesis bolter or a Vindicare Assassin's Exitus
   rifle) then the alarm is immediately raised.
4. **Close combat:** If a sentry _survives_ a round of close combat then the alarm
   is raised. Charging does not raise the alarm.
5. **Bodies:** When a sentry model is killed, lay it on its side instead of removing
   it. If a living sentry moves within spotting distance of a body, the alarm
   is raised.

Once the alarm is raised, remove all sentry models and bodies from the table. They
play no further part in the mission.

## Sustained attack

**The attacker may return destroyed Line units to the battle from Reserves.**

This special rule only applies to the attacker in a scenario. Whenever
a unit belonging to the attacker with the Line (X) rule is destroyed,
that player may choose to place a new unit into reserves. The
new unit must have exactly the same size, wargear options, and so on as
the unit that was destroyed.

If one of the attacker's units with Line (X) gains the Routed status
and is also below 50% of its starting strength, the attacker may choose
to immediately remove that unit from the table and treat it as destroyed.
This allows them to place a new unit into reserves.
